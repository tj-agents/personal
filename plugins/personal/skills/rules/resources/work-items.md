# Personal work items and progress

Owns automatic work-item recording and progress updates for work belonging to an explicitly adopted
personal project. Record established bugs, agent or process mistakes, deliberate debt, and authorized
feature, story or task work through the project's selected GitHub operations without waiting to be
asked. Honor an explicit no-recording limit. Do not invent work merely to populate a backlog.

Resolve the affected source project before applying this policy. Its adoption and evidence determine
the recording decision even when the problem was demonstrated in another checkout. Apply this policy
to that source item without adopting Personal's other rules for the observing repository. Employer-owned
facts stay within their authorized employer context.

Reconcile the canonical identity before creating a record. For repeated observations, append material
new evidence, the known cause and current outcome or repair state. Automatically keep its issue and
repository and owner board entries aligned with verified progress, including work starting, blocking,
handoff, review and accepted completion. Verify remote persistence before claiming an update succeeded.
The selected GitHub operations own their commands, factual validation and persistence checks.

Recording and board maintenance do not authorize, prioritize or dispatch ordinary backlog
implementation. Apply
[Workflow's incidental-problems decision](https://github.com/tj-agents/core/blob/main/.agents/workflow/resources/problem-records.md#incidental-problems)
and preserve the active task and existing repair owner. For a demonstrated defect in an authorized
tj-agents reusable standards or tooling source, this policy grants the bounded source repair routine
owned by `template:self-repair`: reconcile or refine its item, reuse a verified repair owner, or hand
off an eligible unowned incidental repair by default. Template establishes source eligibility and
repair authority independently; an organization name or checkout location establishes neither.

```text
A personal project requests CSV export: record its authorized feature and maintain its board state.
Its CSV parser has an established defect: reconcile that project's bug and update verified progress.
An employer task demonstrates a reusable Core hook defect: apply this policy to the independently
supported Core defect. Template selects its eligible source routine; the employer task retains its
own policy, evidence and owner.
An ordinary recorded debt item remains in the backlog until implementation is authorized.
```
